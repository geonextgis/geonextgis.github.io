About

Full CV

I am a PhD researcher in landscape-scale agroecosystem modelling, specialising in geospatial data science, machine learning and agricultural systems modelling. I develop machine learning, deep learning and hybrid process-based modelling workflows for crop modelling, yield forecasting, crop mapping and climate change impact assessment, using large-scale Earth observation, climate and soil datasets. I am interested in open science, scientific software development, and advancing agricultural science and technology to address real-world challenges, particularly in underserved regions and vulnerable agricultural systems.
